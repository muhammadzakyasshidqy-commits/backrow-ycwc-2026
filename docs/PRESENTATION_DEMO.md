# BACKROW 11.0 — 60-Second Capability Demo

## 0–8 s — show the failure

Show source and audience evidence at the same time.

**“This slide says 2.5%. From the audience position, the captured information becomes 25%. BACKROW tests what survived the room—not only what is in my file.”**

## 8–18 s — judge-controlled setup

Load the PDF once. Place the audience camera at the tested seat. Let the judge choose a slide. Do not provide a page index to the analysis path.

## 18–32 s — exact localized evidence

BACKROW identifies the projected page, rectifies it, and localizes the source region:

- source: `Launch only if error rate stays below 2.5%.`
- audience decode: `...below 25%`;
- result: `AT RISK`.

Say:

**“I do not give the whole slide a vague AI score. I show the exact information that failed.”**

## 32–42 s — show what AI actually does

Open technical evidence briefly:

**“AudienceNet is our custom learned survivability channel. OCR gives direct text evidence. Matching and homography are classical vision. Direct exact contradictions outrank the learned model.”**

## 42–52 s — repair/rescan

Press **Run fix → rescan proof** or make the live repair.

Bundled fallback must move from:

`6 recovered / 2 at risk`

to:

`8 recovered / 0 at risk`

with `2.5%` recovered exactly.

## 52–57 s — fail closed

Use a wrong/ambiguous frame if safe:

**“If BACKROW cannot prove which slide it is seeing, it refuses to invent a result.”**

## 57–60 s — scientific boundary

**“A camera is not a human eye, so the final model claim is tested with a blinded, timed real-projector reader study. I built that measurement path too.”**

Close:

**“Choose a slide.”**

## Live challenge
The preferred live challenge uses two devices: the presenter workstation stays at the front; a judge/audience phone pairs with a six-digit code at the back. The judge can choose the slide/capture. The presenter receives automatic slide matching and evidence without walking to the phone.
