# BACKROW 11.0 — YCWC 2026 Rule Alignment

Verified on **2026-09-25** against the official YCWC 2026 guide linked from Timedoor Academy and the Indonesian guide copy.

## Senior requirements

- Category: **Senior (15–18)**.
- Output: **AI-integrated website**.
- Technology: HTML/CSS/JavaScript, with or without a framework; AI assistance is permitted.
- Submission via the participant's own Google Drive with access enabled (not private).
- Required contents for Senior:
  1. hosted website link in a `.txt` file;
  2. website source folder or `.zip`;
  3. project explanation covering idea, how it works, and unique features, in text or video.
- Maximum one submitted project; if more than one is submitted, the latest is assessed.
- **Paid plugins are prohibited.** The BACKROW submission build uses no paid plugin or paid cloud AI API at runtime.

## Scoring

- **Idea & Creativity: 40%**
- **Coding & AI Implementation: 30%**
- **Presentation: 30%**

Presentation/body-language and PowerPoint/project display criteria apply at Exhibition.

## Demonstration

- Participant must demonstrate the project personally; representation by another person is not allowed.
- Demonstration includes explaining title, idea, and results.
- Estimated presentation/demo duration is 3–10 minutes, subject to each exhibition's technical meeting/rundown.

## BACKROW compliance notes

- Custom AI: AudienceNet 2.0.
- Third-party pretrained AI: Tesseract 5 LSTM OCR.
- Classical CV: ORB, RANSAC/homography, perspective rectification.
- No claim that OCR is the project's custom AI.
- No fake human-readability accuracy is claimed; camera evidence and human eyesight are explicitly separated.
- Presenter rehearsal uses only local browser/OS speech synthesis and captions; no external paid TTS/plugin is part of the submission build.
